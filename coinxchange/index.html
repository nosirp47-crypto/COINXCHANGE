FOUNDER ROADMAP

From Prototype to Platform

Phase 1 · Validate

Launch a landing page and community beta. Test whether people upload coin photos, share discoveries, and return.

Phase 2 · Real Accounts

Add secure authentication, database-backed posts, moderation, image storage, and user profiles.

Phase 3 · Trusted Intelligence

License authoritative numismatic data and integrate carefully evaluated AI-assisted identification with confidence levels.

Phase 4 · Marketplace

Add verified sellers, payment processing, shipping, disputes, tax considerations, and transaction monitoring.
